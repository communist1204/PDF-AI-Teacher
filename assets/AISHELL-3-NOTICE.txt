The five original WAV files are downloaded without audio modification.
Their matching published transcripts have sentence-final punctuation added.
Speaker numbers identify anonymous adult corpus speakers, not celebrities.
These are curated reference voices for local TTS, not a popularity ranking
or an endorsement by the speakers or dataset authors.
Exact filenames, transcripts and checksums are in cosy-voices.json.

The separate cosy_default voice retains the original CosyVoice repository's
zero_shot_prompt.wav. It is not an AISHELL-3 recording.
